隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

中文 English

简短版本

  • 远屏不要求注册账号，不收集、不上传你的个人数据。
  • App 里没有广告、没有统计埋点，也没有第三方统计或崩溃上报 SDK。
  • 远程连接直接从你的设备连到你的电脑，不经过开发者的服务器。开发者不运营任何接收你数据的服务器。
  • 密码和密钥保存在系统钥匙串或安全隔区里，只在这台设备上。

本政策适用于 iOS / iPadOS App「远屏 Remote Screen」（以下称“App”），以及可选安装在电脑上的「Remote Host」程序。App 由个人开发者 Xiaolong Gao 开发和提供（以下称“我们”）。

我们收集的信息

无。我们不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、设备标识、使用行为、画面内容或任何其他个人信息，也不把数据出售或分享给任何人。

数据在哪里传输

你连接电脑时，画面、声音、键盘鼠标输入、剪贴板内容和收发的文件，都在你的设备和你的电脑之间直接传输：

  • 局域网：设备和电脑在同一网络里时直接连接。
  • tailnet（Tailscale / Headscale）：在外网时经你自己的 tailnet 连接，流量由 WireGuard 端到端加密。无法直连时，加密后的流量可能经过你的 tailnet 所配置的中继（DERP）服务器转发，中继看不到内容。这些中继由 Tailscale 或你的 Headscale 管理员提供，不由我们提供。
  • 连接方式与加密：使用 Remote Host 时，连接用 TLS 加密，并用设备密钥双向认证。使用 Windows 远程桌面（RDP）时，使用该协议的 TLS / 网络级身份验证。使用 Mac 屏幕共享（VNC）时，画面数据是否加密取决于 macOS 本身；在外网建议通过 tailnet 连接。

App 会发起的网络连接

除了连接你自己添加的电脑，App 只会在你使用相应功能时连接下面这些地址。它们都不是我们的服务器。

保存在你设备上的数据

  • 电脑列表和设置：保存在 App 自己的存储空间里。
  • 密码、API 令牌：你选择“记住”时保存在系统钥匙串中，设为不随 iCloud 钥匙串同步。
  • 设备身份密钥、SSH 私钥：在支持的设备上由安全隔区（Secure Enclave）生成和保管，私钥不离开设备。
  • 会话录制：只在你开始录制时产生，加密保存在本机；导出与否由你决定。
  • 日志与崩溃记录：只保存在本机，不会自动上传。只有你在诊断页面主动导出并分享时才会离开设备，导出时可以选择把地址打码。

删除 App 会删除它在本机存储空间里的数据；钥匙串中的条目由 iOS 管理。

系统权限

  • 本地网络：用来发现同一网络里打开了屏幕共享的 Mac，以及连接局域网里的电脑。
  • 相机：只用于扫描电脑屏幕上的配对二维码，不拍照、不保存。
  • Face ID：用于解锁 App。验证由 iOS 在设备上完成，我们拿不到你的面容数据。

你可以随时在 iOS 的「设置」里关闭这些权限。

第三方服务

  • Tailscale App：如果你选择用手机上的 Tailscale App 接入，它是一个独立的 App，适用 Tailscale 自己的隐私政策。
  • Headscale / Tailscale 账号：tailnet 的登录与管理由你选择的服务提供方负责，适用其各自的条款与隐私政策。
  • Apple：如果你在 iOS 中开启了“与 App 开发者共享”分析数据，Apple 可能会按其规则向开发者提供匿名的崩溃报告。这由 Apple 处理，可以在 设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进 中关闭。

Remote Host（电脑端，可选）

Remote Host 在你的电脑上运行，只接受你配对过的设备连接。它不向我们发送任何数据；日志和崩溃文件只保存在那台电脑上。

儿童

隐私政策

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

中文 English

The short version

  • Remote Screen requires no account and does not collect or upload your personal data.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Remote connections go directly from your device to your computer and never pass through the developer's servers. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data.
  • Passwords and keys are kept in the system Keychain or the Secure Enclave, on this device only.

This policy covers the iOS / iPadOS app “Remote Screen” (远屏, the “App”) and the optional “Remote Host” program for computers. The App is developed and provided by Xiaolong Gao, an individual developer (“we”).

Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, device identifiers, usage data, screen content, or any other personal information, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Where your data travels

When you connect to a computer, the screen image, audio, keyboard and pointer input, clipboard contents, and transferred files travel directly between your device and your computer:

  • Local network: a direct connection when both are on the same network.
  • Tailnet (Tailscale / Headscale): away from home, traffic goes over your own tailnet and is end-to-end encrypted by WireGuard. When a direct path is not available, the encrypted traffic may be forwarded by relay (DERP) servers configured for your tailnet, which cannot read it. These relays are run by Tailscale or by your Headscale administrator, not by us.
  • Protocols and encryption: with Remote Host, connections use TLS with mutual device-key authentication. With Windows Remote Desktop (RDP), the protocol's TLS / Network Level Authentication is used. With Mac Screen Sharing (VNC), whether screen data is encrypted depends on macOS; over the internet we recommend connecting through a tailnet.

Network connections the App makes

Apart from the computers you add, the App only connects to the following, and only when you use the related feature. None of them are our servers.

Data stored on your device

  • Computer list and settings: stored in the App's own storage.
  • Passwords and API tokens: stored in the system Keychain when you choose “Remember”, marked not to sync through iCloud Keychain.
  • Device identity key and SSH private keys: generated and held by the Secure Enclave on supported devices; private keys never leave the device.
  • Session recordings: created only when you start a recording, stored encrypted on the device; exporting is up to you.
  • Logs and crash records: kept on the device only and never uploaded automatically. They leave the device only if you export and share them from the diagnostics screen, with an option to mask addresses.

Deleting the App removes the data in its own storage; Keychain items are managed by iOS.

System permissions

  • Local Network: to discover Macs with Screen Sharing turned on and to connect to computers on your local network.
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code shown on a computer's screen; nothing is captured or saved.
  • Face ID: to unlock the App. iOS performs the check on the device; we never receive your face data.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in iOS Settings.

Third-party services

  • Tailscale app: if you choose to connect through the Tailscale app on your phone, it is a separate app governed by Tailscale's own privacy policy.
  • Headscale / Tailscale accounts: tailnet sign-in and administration are handled by the provider you choose, under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Apple: if you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in iOS, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer under Apple's rules. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Remote Host (optional, for computers)

Remote Host runs on your computer and only accepts connections from devices you have paired. It sends no data to us; its logs and crash files stay on that computer.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes to how data is handled will also be noted in the App's release notes.
